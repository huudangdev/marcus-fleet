# Design Review Contract

Use this reference when executing multi-role design critiques and scorecards.

## 1. Roles
- Design Lead (Aesthetics & Tokens)
- Frontend Engineer (Feasibility & State)
- Product Manager (Business Goals & Flows)
- QA Lead (Accessibility & Edge Cases)

## 2. Threshold
All scorecards must achieve $\ge 85\%$ rating before sign-off.
